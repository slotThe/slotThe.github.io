Tofu Omelette

Prep time: 5mins Servings: 2 Cooking time: 20mins Macros per serving: 332kcal 31c 11f 27p

Ingredients§

  • 85g chickpea flour
  • Water
  • 200g tofu
  • 4tbsp yeast flakes
  • 1tsp turmeric
  • 1tsp garlic powder
  • 1tsp onion powder
  • 1tsp baking powder
  • Some vegetables of choice
  • 30g vegan cheese
  • Salt & pepper
  • Oil

Instructions§

  • Sauté vegetables in pan.
  • Blend tofu with enough water until smooth.
  • Combine all dry ingredients together, and add the blended tofu to the mix. Might need to add some water; texture should be around that of pancakes.
  • Heat pan on medium to high heat. Oil use depends on the pan, use a lot if it’s old :).
  • Cook until the edges start to firm up and the top looks dry (like 4 minutes).
  • Put vegetables and optionally some vegan cheese on top.
  • Fold in half—this is the part where you’ll know if you used enough oil.
  • Cook for another 3–5 minutes.

Notes§

  • Macros are with around 5ml oil per omelette, might need more depending on the stickiness of the pan.
