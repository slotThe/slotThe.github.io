# Ingredients

+ 85g chickpea flour
+ Water
+ 200g tofu
+ 4tbsp yeast flakes
+ 1tsp turmeric
+ 1tsp garlic powder
+ 1tsp onion powder
+ 1tsp baking powder
+ Some vegetables of choice
+ 30g vegan cheese
+ Salt & pepper
+ Oil

# Instructions

1. Sauté the vegetables in a pan.
2. Blend the tofu with water until smooth.
3. Combine all dry ingredients and add the blended tofu to the mix.
   Might need to add additional water; texture should be around that of pancake batter.
4. Heat pan on medium to high heat. Oil use depends on the pan, use a lot if it's old :).
5. Cook until the edges start to firm up and the top looks dry (like 4 minutes).
6. Put vegetables and optionally some vegan cheese on top.
7. Fold in half—this is the part where you'll know if you used enough oil.
8. Cook for another 3–5 minutes.

# Notes

+ Macros are with around 5ml oil per omelette, might need more depending on the stickiness of the pan.
